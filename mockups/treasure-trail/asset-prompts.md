# Realistic asset prompts

All assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The PNG originals remain alongside index.html and are embedded in the HTML for the single-file Render staging build.

- `island-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic broad low tropical island, sea-level view, long horizontal sandy shoreline, lush palms and jungle, weathered rocks, natural daylight, transparent background. No rainbow, castle, text, people, or plane.
- `rishi-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic cinematic game character transparent cutout. Full-body rear view of a fictional young Indian boy with dark swept hair, cream kurta and loose trousers, lavender diagonal sash tied at the waist, bare feet. Arms extended for balance, facing away, natural fabric folds and realistic proportions. No scenery, ground, text, front face, or backpack.
- `rock-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic low-profile ocean stepping rock, viewed slightly from above the front. Broad, nearly horizontal pale gray weathered top suitable for game numbers, rugged basalt sides, wet edges and natural geological textures. Isolated transparent background, no ocean, numbers, or text.
- `ocean-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic panoramic ocean, camera just above the surface facing a distant horizon. Cloudy daylight sky above deep teal water with many rolling swells, foamy crests, sea spray, wind-driven surface and natural light. Large foreground waves, smaller waves at the horizon. No island, land, boats, plane, people, rocks, structures, rainbow, or text.

## Islands of Courage worlds

The following additional assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and saved as original PNGs beside index.html:

- `lava-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic cinematic fantasy ocean of molten lava, low distant horizon, orange-red currents and dark cooling crust, subtle volcanic smoke and charcoal-purple twilight sky; open central route, no castle, characters, dragons, UI or text.
- `sky-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic cinematic twilight sky above an ocean of luminous clouds, silver-blue lavender atmosphere, distant tiny floating rock formations at the edges; open central route, no castle, characters, UI or text.
- `castle-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic inviting medieval castle, warm-lit windows, tall stone towers, slate roofs and purple banners, on a broad craggy rocky island with a stone arrival courtyard; complete ground-level frontal cutout on a transparent background, no sky, water, lava, people or text.
- `creatures-realistic-v1.png`: Photorealistic transparent three-column sprite atlas: complete left-facing silver bottlenose dolphin in the left third, orange-and-blue tropical reef fish in the middle third, and bronze fantasy dragon with spread wings in the right third. Separate subjects, no overlap or background, no grid lines or text. Rendered in the game by source-region selection from the original atlas.
